01 · Problem

Small businesses buy traffic before fixing the last 10 feet.

Phone-only scheduling, wrong currency labels, vague CTAs, weak trust proof and generic positioning silently waste high-intent visitors. Traditional audits are either static checklists or black-box LLM reports.

02 · Solution

A conversion audit you can inspect.

Paste the visible homepage copy. LeakLens produces a 0–100 conversion-health score, ranked evidence-backed findings, and prioritized remediation.

04 · Technical architecture

AI where it helps. Rules where they’re better.

Deterministic layer

Observable evidence

CTA language, online booking, forms, price markers, currency context, social proof and differentiation.

Semantic layer

Browser-side AI

MobileBERT MNLI via Transformers.js classifies friction, trust, CTA strength and positioning.

Fusion layer

Confidence thresholds

Semantic risks affect the audit only when model confidence clears explicit thresholds.

05 · Responsible AI

Private by design. No prompt theater.

Local inference

Business copy is not sent to an LLM provider.

Fallback works

The product remains useful in rules-only mode if the model cannot load.

Confidence ≠ fact

AI findings are labeled as risk signals and paired with human-readable evidence.

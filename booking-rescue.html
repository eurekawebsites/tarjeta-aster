What gets fixed

The exact implementation depends on the site, but the sprint is deliberately narrow: remove friction at the moment a visitor is trying to become a lead or appointment.

01

Booking path

A clear mobile-first route from service page to appointment request, without forcing visitors to hunt for the next step.

02

Forms + fallbacks

Verify the inquiry flow, improve field logic and confirmations, and add sensible call, text, or email fallbacks where appropriate.

03

Service routing

Capture the service, date preference, and the minimum information your team needs to respond quickly.

04

Mobile cleanup

Fix the high-friction mobile moments around calls to action, forms, buttons, spacing, and the booking handoff.

05

Confirmation

Give visitors an unmistakable success state and next-step expectation instead of leaving them wondering whether a request went through.

06

Measurement

Basic event tracking so you can see whether visitors start or complete the booking/inquiry path. No invented ROI claims.
